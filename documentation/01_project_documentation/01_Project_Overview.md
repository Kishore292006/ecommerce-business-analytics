# E-Commerce Business Analytics & Customer Intelligence

## Project Overview

An end-to-end e-commerce analytics project using MySQL and Power BI to evaluate sales performance, customer purchasing behavior, product categories, payment patterns, and delivery experience using the Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset by Olist.

## Business Objective

Analyze historical e-commerce transactions to identify revenue drivers, understand customer purchasing patterns, evaluate delivery performance, and uncover operational issues that may affect customer satisfaction.

## Business Questions

- How do sales and order volumes change over the available period?
- Which product categories contribute the most revenue?
- How does repeat-customer behavior differ from one-time purchasing?
- Which states have a higher freight burden relative to product revenue?
- How do delivery delays relate to customer review scores?
- What patterns appear in cancellations and unavailable orders?

## Dataset

**Source:** Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset by Olist, available on Kaggle.

The dataset contains related tables covering orders, customers, order items, products, payments, reviews, sellers, geolocation, and product-category translations.

The data represents historical transactions. Incomplete periods, table relationships, and data-quality discrepancies are considered when interpreting results.

## Technology Stack

- **MySQL 8.0:** Relational data analysis and business queries.
- **SQL:** Joins, aggregations, date analysis, KPI calculations, and validation.
- **Power BI:** Interactive business reporting and visualization.
- **Git and GitHub:** Version control and project publication.
- **Markdown:** Technical documentation.

## Analytical Scope

1. Sales performance and trends
2. Product and category revenue
3. Customer purchasing behavior
4. Geographic performance and freight burden
5. Payment methods
6. Delivery performance
7. Customer reviews and satisfaction
8. Order status and cancellations
9. Data-quality validation

## Deliverables

- Organized SQL scripts for business analysis.
- Documented analytical findings and data-quality checks.
- Four-page Power BI report covering executive sales performance, product categories, customer and geographic insights, and delivery and customer experience.
- Project documentation and a GitHub README.

## Limitations

The dataset is historical and has incomplete coverage in some periods. Review records do not always correspond one-to-one with orders. Observed relationships between delivery performance and customer reviews do not establish causation, and the causes of individual data discrepancies cannot always be determined from the available data.

## Outcome

The project demonstrates a structured approach to transforming relational e-commerce data into validated business metrics, interpretable findings, and actionable recommendations. Final conclusions are based on the completed analysis and are documented with relevant caveats.
